Rest timer

What Android allows (please read)
  • The countdown itself is always correct: it's based on the end time, so locking the phone or switching apps never makes it drift.
  • With the app open on screen, the sound, vibration and on-screen alert are reliable.
  • With the phone locked or the app in the background, Android and Chrome may pause web apps to save battery. The background notification and chime are a best effort — they may arrive late or not at all. Vibration from the page itself only works while the app is on screen (the notification can still vibrate).
  • Samsung battery settings can make this worse. For best results: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Battery → Unrestricted, and allow Chrome notifications.
  • Rests longer than about 5 minutes are less likely to notify in the background.
  • If you reopen the app after the rest ended, it shows "Rest finished" instead of beeping late.

Rest time per exercise

Seconds of rest after each completed set. Saved per workout day, so the same exercise can have different rest on different days. You can also change these inside each exercise during a workout.

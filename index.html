What Android allows
  • The countdown is always correct — it uses the end time, so locking the phone never makes it drift.
  • With VOLT on screen, sound, vibration and the on-screen alert are reliable.
  • With the phone locked or VOLT in the background, Android may pause web apps. The notification and chime are best effort and can be late or missing. Page vibration only works on screen (the notification can still vibrate).
  • Helps: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Battery → Unrestricted, and allow Chrome notifications.
  • Rests over ~5 minutes are less likely to notify in the background. Reopening after rest ended shows "Rest finished" instead of beeping late.
